How it works

AlgoDance is a crowd-built movement dictionary. Invent a short move, teach it a word or phrase, then speak by dancing. Everything stays on this device; nothing is filmed or uploaded.

1 TYPE a word 2 RIGHT hand on your face, hold for 3-2-1 3 DANCE one short move, four counts 4 LEFT hand on your face saves it
The fine print

The ghost on the video is dancing the dictionary's first move, AlgoDance. Copy it and its word fires; that is the whole idea. Your moves are saved in this browser, so they are still here tomorrow. The hand-on-face moments are trimmed off every recording, so only the move itself is kept.

Teach a move

Ready. Type or pick a word, then press Record movement.

R RIGHT hand on your face, hold for 3-2-1 4 DANCE one short move, four counts L LEFT hand on your face saves it

The R and L are printed on your wrists on screen. Record the same word again to add takes; three takes match best.

The dictionary

Every move lives in this browser, so it is still here tomorrow. Export saves them all as a small file of skeleton coordinates, never video; Import adds a friend's file to yours. Click a word (or Play) to dance along with its ghost.

    About AlgoDance

    AlgoDance turns movement into words. Teach it a move for a word; dance the move, and the word is shown and spoken.

    Your privacy

    Everything happens locally, in your browser, on your device.

    • No data is sent to us. There is no server, no account, and no analytics. We never see your camera, your movements, or your words.
    • Your camera stays on your device. Video is processed frame by frame in the browser to detect body pose and is never uploaded, recorded, or stored. No video ever leaves your computer.
    • Only coordinates are saved. A saved move is just a short sequence of skeleton coordinates, stored in your browser's local storage. No images.
    • You are in control. Export or delete your moves anytime in the Codes tab. Clearing your browser storage removes everything.
    • Third-party models. The pose models (MediaPipe BlazePose, and optionally MoveNet) download once from public CDNs so detection can run on your device. That is a normal file download; your camera data is not part of it. Everything else the page needs, including its fonts, is served from this site, so opening it tells no one else you did.

    How it works

    Pose tracking runs in the browser (WebAssembly / WebGPU). Movements are compared with Dynamic Time Warping so tempo does not matter, and matching happens entirely on your device.
